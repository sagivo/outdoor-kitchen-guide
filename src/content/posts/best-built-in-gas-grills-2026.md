A built-in grill is the anchor of an outdoor kitchen, and it is the one component you cannot easily swap later — the island is literally cut around it. This guide compares the five built-in heads worth buying in 2026, from the warranty king to the value pick.

[[chart:btu]]

## Our top picks at a glance

**Best overall: [Blaze Premium LTE 32](amazon:blaze-premium-lte-32).** Four cast stainless burners, 60,000 BTU, heat zone separators, and a lifetime body warranty. The rational choice for a permanent install.

**Best for rotisserie: [Napoleon Prestige 500 Built-In](amazon:napoleon-prestige-500-built-in).** Infrared rear burner, thick 9mm grids, and a drop-in trim ring. Costs more, cooks more ways.

**Best value: [Bull Outlaw 30](amazon:bull-outlaw-30).** 304 stainless, 60,000 BTU, and a rear infrared rotisserie burner for hundreds less than the big names.

## How we rank built-in grills

We score on published specs, warranty terms, and owner reports — never on commissions. What matters most: **materials** (304 stainless resists rust far better than 430), **burner output per square inch**, **warranty length on the body**, and **features you will actually use** (rotisserie, infrared, lighting). ## What to know before you buy

**Cutout first, grill second.** Every brand publishes different cutout dimensions. Finalize the grill before your contractor cuts stone — a 1-inch mistake is a very expensive mistake.

**Budget the island, not just the head.** The grill head is typically 30–50% of the project cost. Our [outdoor kitchen cost estimator](/tools/build-cost-estimator/) prices the full build so the head doesn't blow the budget.

**Gas line sizing matters.** High-BTU heads need properly sized gas lines. Mention the grill model to your installer before the line is run, not after. And decide your fuel now — our [natural gas vs propane guide](/posts/natural-gas-vs-propane-grill/) covers the cost and convenience trade-offs, since most heads aren't convertible after purchase.

**Not sure about the island at all?** Read [built-in vs freestanding](/posts/built-in-vs-freestanding-grill/) before committing to construction — a quality cart grill delivers 90% of the cooking for a fraction of the project cost.

Most buyers should choose between the Blaze (warranty, longevity), the Napoleon (versatility), and the Bull (value). Read the [full reviews](/grills/) for the details, then measure twice.
