Ooni and Gozney own the residential pizza oven market, and the rivalry is genuinely useful: Ooni pushes value and variety, Gozney pushes thermal engineering. Here's how the lineups compare.

## The lineups

**Ooni:** Koda 16 (gas, 16", ~$549) · Koda 12 (gas, 12") · Karu 12 (multi-fuel, ~$379) · Karu 16 (multi-fuel, 16"). Breadth is the strategy — every budget, every fuel.

**Gozney:** Roccbox (~$499, 12", gas + optional wood) · Dome S1 (~$1,499, 16", gas) · Dome (~$1,999, dual-fuel). Fewer models, each engineered harder.

## Head-to-head matchups

**Koda 16 vs Roccbox:** The most common dilemma. The Roccbox cooks a better 12-inch pizza — the rolling-flame baffle gives more even top heat, and it recovers faster between bakes. The Koda 16 cooks bigger pizzas and more of them per night. Feeding 2–4: Roccbox. Feeding 6+: Koda 16.

**Karu 12 vs Roccbox:** Wood flavor (Karu) vs gas convenience (Roccbox) at similar prices. The Karu is more fun and more work; the Roccbox is more consistent. Beginners who romanticize wood fire should be honest about whether they will tend it.

**Koda 16 vs Dome S1:** The step-up question. The Dome S1's heat management and control are meaningfully better — but it costs roughly 3x the Koda 16. Worth it only if pizza night is a weekly ritual, not a monthly one.

## Ecosystems

Ooni has the bigger accessory ecosystem: more peels, covers, tables, and third-party add-ons. Gozney's accessories are fewer but excellent. Both brands hold resale value well — used Roccboxes and Kodas sell fast.

## The bottom line

Buy Ooni for value and choice, Gozney for peak performance. Either way, you are getting a 950°F oven — the pizza will be good. See the [best pizza ovens of 2026](/posts/best-pizza-ovens-2026/) for the full ranking.
