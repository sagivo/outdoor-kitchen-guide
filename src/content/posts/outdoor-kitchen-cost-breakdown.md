The grill head gets all the attention, but it is usually less than half the project cost. Here is where the money actually goes in a 2026 outdoor kitchen build.

[[diagram:island]]

## The line items

**Grill head: $1,900–$3,200.** A quality 30–32 inch built-in head. This is the [comparison chart](/grills/) — don't cheap out here, because everything else is built around it.

**Island/base construction: $2,500–$6,000.** Framing, cement board, stone or stucco veneer. L-shaped islands cost notably more than straight runs — every corner adds labor.

**Countertop: $800–$3,000.** Concrete and tile are the budget plays; granite and quartzite are the premium ones. Outdoor-rated material matters — indoor granite can fail in freeze-thaw climates.

**Gas line: $500–$1,500.** Running a natural gas line to the island, or less if your meter is nearby. Propane avoids this entirely (tank instead), at the cost of refills.

**Electrical: $300–$1,000.** Outlets, lighting, and possibly a 240V run for an electric pizza oven or heater.

**Refrigeration/sink (optional): $1,000–$3,000.** Outdoor-rated fridge, sink, and plumbing. The fastest way to double a budget.

**Labor: 30–50% on top.** If you're not DIY-ing the island, labor is the silent budget-killer. Get the quote before buying the grill.

## Three realistic budgets

- **Essential grill island (~$8,000–$12,000):** mid-range head (Bull Outlaw, Coyote S-Series), straight island, concrete counter, propane.
- **The sweet spot (~$15,000–$22,000):** premium head (Blaze, Napoleon), L-island, granite, natural gas line, lighting.
- **Full outdoor kitchen (~$25,000–$40,000+):** everything above plus a [pizza oven](/posts/best-pizza-ovens-2026/) like the Gozney Dome S1, outdoor fridge, and sink.

## Where to save without regret

1. **Straight island, not L-shaped.** Corners are expensive.
2. **Concrete or tile countertop.** Looks great, costs half of granite.
3. **Propane instead of a gas line** — if your grill usage is moderate.
4. **Phase the appliances.** Build the island with space for a pizza oven or fridge, add them next year.
