The Blaze Premium LTE 32 is the rationalist's built-in: no infrared burner, no rotisserie theatrics, just 304 stainless steel, 60,000 BTU, and a lifetime body warranty. It is the grill BBQGuys picked as their best overall built-in, and the reasons are all about longevity.

## Who the Premium LTE 32 is for (and who should skip it)

It fits the buyer building a permanent outdoor kitchen who thinks in decades. If the grill will live outside year-round — especially near the coast, where the 316L marine-grade option matters — the warranty and materials are the point.

Skip it if you want rotisserie or infrared searing: neither is on offer at this price. The Napoleon Prestige 500 gives you both for less money, trading away the warranty.

## Heat zones and everyday cooking

The heat zone separators are the standout cooking feature: dividers between burners let you hold one zone at searing heat while another idles low for warming or gentle cooking. In practice this replaces some of what an infrared burner does — you get a hot zone and a cool zone without juggling. The double-lined hood keeps temperatures stable in wind, which matters more than most buyers expect.

## Build quality and the warranty

Everything about the LTE reads as overbuilt: heavy-gauge 304 stainless, a hood with an assist system that doesn't slam, LED-illuminated controls for night grilling. The lifetime warranty on the body is genuinely best-in-class — most rivals offer 10–15 years. Register the grill and keep your receipt; warranty claims need both.

## Ownership over a decade

The lifetime warranty changes the ownership math in a way spec sheets don't capture. Burners, heat tents, and igniters are wear items on every grill — but on the Blaze, the expensive structural parts (body, hood, firebox) are covered for life, so your decade-two costs are limited to consumables. Compare that to a mid-range head you replace entirely at year eight. The least exciting grill here is also the safest long-term bet. Buy the Blaze when the outdoor kitchen is a 15-year investment, not a 5-year one.
