The Napoleon Prestige 500 Built-In is the grill head for buyers who want one head that does everything: hard searing, rotisserie, and everyday grilling. Four main burners plus an infrared rear burner give it a wider cooking repertoire than most rivals at this size.

## Who the Prestige 500 Built-In is for (and who should skip it)

It fits the builder planning a permanent outdoor kitchen around a 32-inch opening who grills often enough to appreciate rotisserie cooking — whole chickens, roasts, and ribs on a spit are where this grill separates from simpler heads.

Skip it if rotisserie means nothing to you: a Blaze Premium LTE gives you a better warranty and zone separators for similar money, and a Bull Outlaw saves you several hundred dollars. Skip it too if your island is already cut — measure the opening first, because 32-inch heads are not interchangeable across brands.

## Burners, heat, and cooking performance

The four main burners deliver strong, even heat across 760 square inches of cooking area, and the 9mm stainless grids hold temperature well when you load the grates with cold meat. The infrared rear burner is the headline feature: it runs hot enough for genuine rotisserie browning, not just slow warming.

## Build and installation

Napoleon's fit and finish is a half-step above most mid-range built-ins — solid welds, smooth lid action, and the SafetyGlow knobs that turn red when a burner is on. The integrated trim ring is genuinely useful: it frames the cutout and hides the small gaps that make DIY islands look amateur. Ventilation still matters — leave the clearances the manual specifies and never enclose a propane tank without dedicated vents.

## The bottom line

You are paying for versatility and finish. If the rotisserie burner will get used monthly, the Prestige 500 Built-In earns its premium. If not, put the savings toward the countertop.
