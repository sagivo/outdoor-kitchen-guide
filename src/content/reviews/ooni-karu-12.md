The Ooni Karu 12 is the cheapest way to cook real wood-fired pizza: 950°F on wood or charcoal, folding legs, and 26.4 pounds. It is also the most hands-on oven in this guide — fire is a skill, not a setting.

## Who the Karu 12 is for (and who should skip it)

It fits the flavor-first buyer who finds fire management fun rather than tedious. If you have cooked over wood before — grill, fire pit, smoker — the learning curve is short.

Skip it if you want push-button pizza. The Koda 16 gives you 950°F with zero fire tending, and for weeknight cooking that convenience wins for most people.

## Fire management basics

Start the fire 15 minutes before you want to bake, feed small splits of hardwood, and learn to read the stone: too hot and the crust chars before the cheese melts, too cool and you get pale, floppy pies. An infrared thermometer is the best $30 accessory you can buy for any pizza oven.

## Portability

At 26.4 pounds with folding legs and a locking chimney, the Karu 12 is the only oven here you will genuinely take to a friend's house. That portability is a real feature, not a spec-sheet line.

## The bottom line

Maximum flavor per dollar, minimum convenience. Know which side of that trade you are on before buying.
