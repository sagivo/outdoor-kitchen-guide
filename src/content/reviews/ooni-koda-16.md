The Ooni Koda 16 is the default pizza oven recommendation for a reason: 16-inch Neapolitan pizzas at 950°F with the convenience of a gas knob. No fire management, no learning curve beyond dough and launching.

## Who the Koda 16 is for (and who should skip it)

It fits families and anyone who hosts. The 16-inch deck is the real upgrade — cooking for six means six launches instead of twelve, which is the difference between enjoying the party and working it.

Skip it if wood-fired flavor is the whole point; this is a gas oven, full stop. Skip it too if you only cook for two — the Koda 12 does the same job for less money and less patio space.

## Heat and cooking performance

The L-shaped flame is a genuine improvement over the original Koda's rear-only burner: heat wraps across the stone more evenly, so the back of the pizza doesn't incinerate while the front stays pale. Twenty minutes to 950°F is honest, and recovery between bakes is fast enough for back-to-back 60-second Margheritas.

## Living with it

At 40 pounds with folding legs, the Koda 16 moves between storage and patio without drama. The stone stays in the oven; brush it clean after it cools. A cover is worth buying — the powder-coated shell handles weather, but the burner and ignition last longer dry.

## The bottom line

The easiest path to great pizza at home. Gas convenience, 16-inch capacity, proven reliability — this is the one to beat.
