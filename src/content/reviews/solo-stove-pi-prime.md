The Solo Stove Pi Prime is the low-risk entry into 950°F pizza: the cheapest gas oven from a major brand that still hits real Neapolitan temperatures. It answers one question — is pizza-oven life for me? — for the least money.

## Who the Pi Prime is for (and who should skip it)

It fits renters, small patios, and the pizza-curious. If you are not sure you will use an oven weekly, this is the smart first buy — and if you outgrow it, you have learned exactly what to look for in the upgrade.

Skip it if you already know you are serious: the small opening gets annoying fast when you are launching your twentieth pizza. Buy the Koda 16 once instead of twice.

## The demi-dome design

The half-dome shape is not just styling — less internal volume means faster preheats (about 15 minutes) and lower propane consumption. The tradeoff is headroom: turning a pizza inside is tighter than in a full-dome oven.

## The bottom line

The cheapest honest 950°F oven. Perfect first oven, outgrown second oven — and that is fine.
